About PMDriven

Life Runs on Projects. Learn to Run Them!

PMDriven is an independent project management education platform dedicated to making project management knowledge practical, accessible, and applicable to real-world challenges.

We believe effective project management goes beyond memorizing frameworks, processes, and terminology. It requires sound judgment, critical thinking, leadership, and informed decision-making.

What We Offer

  • PMP® Exam Preparation: Scenario-based questions, detailed explanations, and practical decision-making strategies.
  • Practical Project Management: Articles, frameworks, and real-world examples that connect theory with practice.
  • Leadership & Power Skills: Learning resources focused on communication, conflict resolution, negotiation, change management, and team leadership.
  • Project Management Scenario Compass: An educational podcast exploring project management scenarios, decisions, and practical lessons.
  • Professional Development: Self-directed learning resources that may support eligible Professional Development Unit (PDU) activities, subject to PMI's requirements.

The Experience Behind PMDriven

PMDriven was founded by a PMP®-certified professional who holds an MBA with a focus on leadership and a Master of Arts in Public Administration, with an academic focus on change management and good governance.

The founder brings extensive project management and leadership experience across public and private sectors, alongside experience as a fellow, coach, and international mentor in leadership and management through the United Nations Institute for Training and Research (UNITAR).

Our Mission

To help project professionals develop the knowledge, judgment, and confidence to navigate real-world challenges, pursue professional certification, and lead effectively.
