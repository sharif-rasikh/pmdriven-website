Project Management Scenario Compass

Our educational podcast with real project challenges, decision-making, and lessons learned.

Learning doesn't stop at reading. Through our podcast, Project Management Scenario Compass, we explore real-world project challenges, difficult decisions, and practical leadership strategies.

From PMP-style situational scenarios to in-depth discussions on communication, conflict resolution, stakeholder management, and Agile leadership, our episodes help project professionals strengthen their judgment and apply what they learn.

Eligible PMI certification holders may also be able to claim Education PDUs for qualifying podcast learning activities, subject to PMI's Continuing Certification Requirements.

About PMDriven

Meet your guide and learn the mission behind PMDriven.

PMDriven was founded on a simple principle: Life Runs on Projects. Learn to Run Them!

Your guide is PMP® Certified, an MBA holder, and has extensive experience as a fellow, coach, and international mentor in leadership and management through the United Nations Institute for Training and Research (UNITAR). Backed by years of hands-on project management and leadership experience across both the public and private sectors, PMDriven bridges the gap between theory and execution.

Drawing from real-world project environments, we cut through the theoretical clutter to teach you the practical, situational mindset required to excel on the PMP exam and lead high-performing teams with confidence.
